How it works

Set it up once in about a minute. After that it runs by itself.

  1. Log in with Kick

    Use your Kick account. clipQ finds your channel and connects to its chat.

  2. Open the queue

    Flip the switch, or have a mod type !cq open. Now viewers can post links in chat.

  3. Watch on stream

    Clips line up in order. Press Next, or let them play one after another automatically.

Built for live chat

Chat is fast and messy. clipQ keeps the queue fair and the stream safe.

Fair order

Clips play in the order they were posted, even when some links take longer to load.

Spam limits

Each viewer can only have a few clips waiting, the same clip can't be added twice, and you choose who can post each platform: subs, OGs, VIPs or mods.

Mods stay in control

Ban or time out a viewer in clipQ and their links are ignored. Deleted messages and Kick bans remove clips right away.

Plays by itself

Kick, YouTube and TikTok clips go to the next one automatically when they end.

Always listening

clipQ reads your chat on its server, so links are collected even if the dashboard isn't open.

Free

Chat commands for you and your mods

Run the queue without leaving chat.

!cq open
Start taking clips from chat
!cq close
Stop taking clips
!cq next
Play the next clip
!cq skip
Skip the current clip
!cq prev
Replay the previous clip
!cq clear
Empty the queue
!cq timeout name 10m
Ignore someone's links for a while
!cq ban name
Ignore someone's links until unbanned (!cq unban name)

Questions

Is clipQ free?

Yes. clipQ is free to use. Donations help pay for hosting.

Which links does clipQ accept?

Kick clips, YouTube videos and Shorts, Twitch clips, TikTok, Instagram Reels and posts, X (Twitter) posts and Streamable.

Does the dashboard have to stay open to collect clips?

No. clipQ reads your Kick chat on its server, so links are collected while the queue is open even if the dashboard tab is closed.

How does clipQ stop spam?

Each viewer can only have a few clips waiting, the same clip can't be in the queue twice, and clips are removed when a moderator deletes the message or bans the viewer.

Is it safe to log in with Kick?

Yes. clipQ uses Kick's official login and only asks to read your profile and channel. It never sees your password and can't post or change anything on your account.
